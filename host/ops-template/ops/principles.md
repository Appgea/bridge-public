# Principles — standing rules index

One terse line per always-on rule. Permanent: append, don't churn. This file is read on every
question, so every line here costs context on every turn — keep it to rules that actually change
what an agent does.

Format: `- [P-NN] <rule>`

## Rules

<!-- Three to start you off. Delete them if they are not yours; they are examples of the SHAPE,
     not a doctrine you inherited. -->

- [P-01] When unsure what something means, ask before writing — never invent. Proceeding under
  ambiguity is fine; hiding the assumption is not.
- [P-02] Never act unattended on consequential, outward-facing or irreversible work — store
  submits, prices, releases, spend. Human checkpoint first.
- [P-03] Default to autonomy on small, reversible, in-plan work. Act and report. P-02 and P-03 are
  the two sides of one rule.
