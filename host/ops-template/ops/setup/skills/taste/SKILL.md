# Taste

Model, weights and calibration: `ops/_system/taste.md`. Read it each run. Brief rules: `ops/_system/design-prompt.md` when present.
Obvious bad design never reaches him. He is the layer for taste, not competence.

## Gate, automatic

One cheap pass on any UI, flow, screenshot, hook or copy, before shipping and before any question.
Contrast, alignment, hierarchy, clutter, dead end, missing state, extra taps, hidden primary action: fix it, note one line.
Nothing subjective left: report the fixes and stop. Never invent a taste question.

## Decide, human-gated

Only for a real judgment, brand or feel call that passed the gate.
Score candidates on the six axes with current weights, one terse reason each. Flag anti-pattern hits.
A close or consequential call gets a small panel of distinct lenses, two lines each. Otherwise one proxy pass.

    DECISION: <one line>   [gate: fixed <n>]
    CANDIDATES: A <label>, B <label>
    PROXY PICK: <A> · confidence <low|med|high> · <why>
    WATCH-OUT: <where this needs your eye>
    DRAFT: "<1-2 lines in his voice>"
    → approve or override in one line.

Confidence is low when the call rests on craft you cannot see.

## Learn

An override or clear reaction appends one calibration line to `taste.md`. A reliable reject becomes an anti-pattern.
A repeating axis preference proposes a weight nudge, asked once. Appends are silent. Never interrogate.
